Quick Help

Click on "Book a Repair" in the sidebar, select your service, device type, choose a date and time, then confirm your booking.

We accept GCash for online payments and C.A.R (Cash After Repair) for in-store payments.

Yes, you can cancel pending or confirmed bookings from your "My Bookings" page. Click the cancel button on the booking you wish to cancel.

C.A.R stands for Cash After Repair. You only pay after the repair is completed and you're satisfied with the service.

Each time slot can accommodate up to 5 bookings to ensure quality service. If a slot is full, please choose another time.
