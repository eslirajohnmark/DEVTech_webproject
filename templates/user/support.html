      Quick answers

      How do I book a repair?

      Open Service Request, pick the service for your device, describe the problem, choose a date and time, and confirm. You can also start from the Troubleshooting assistant.

      How can I pay?

      Pay by QR Ph (scan with GCash, Maya or your bank app; confirmed automatically) or by card. For repairs you can also choose C.A.R, Cash After Repair. Shop orders can be paid cash on delivery.

      Can I cancel a booking or order?

      Yes, from My Bookings or My Orders while it hasn't started. Online payments are refunded in full to the card or e-wallet you used.

      How do I track my repair?

      Monitor Device shows each stage live, from diagnosis to ready for pickup, with notes from your technician.

      What is C.A.R?

      Cash After Repair: you pay at the shop once the repair is done and you've checked your device.
